Myan OCR — live demo

Upload an image with printed Myanmar or English text and extract the text, right here in your browser. Or tap “Try the sample image” to see it work straight away.

How this demo works: it runs Tesseract OCR inside your browser (Tesseract.js) — your image never leaves your device. The full app on GitHub runs the same Tesseract engine on a Python/FastAPI backend and adds PDF pages, batch images, camera tools, detected-text boxes and optional translation, so results can differ from this demo. Always check the extracted text against the original image.
